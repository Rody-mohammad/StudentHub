## Image Requirements

- Format: JPG or WebP
- Size: ~1200px wide max, under 200KB each
- No close-up faces or recognizable people
- No brand logos, readable signs, license plates, or text
- No watermarks
- No dark or gloomy images
- Prefer bright, natural, calm, slightly warm and blue-friendly tones
- Landscape orientation
- Free license (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay)
